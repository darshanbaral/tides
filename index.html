Data sources & how to read this chart

Separate tide panels: Palo Alto Yacht Harbor (9414525) and Coyote Creek (9414575) predicted tides, relative to each station’s Mean Lower Low Water (MLLW). Local datums are not a common absolute elevation reference.

Lower panel: Δ = observed − predicted at Redwood City (9414523) and San Francisco (9414290), calculated only where NOAA supplies both values at the exact same UTC timestamp. Positive means higher than predicted. The panels have separate vertical scales.

Thresholds: The light yellow background marks 9–10 ft at Palo Alto and 10–11 ft at Coyote Creek. Orange extends from the upper threshold to the top of each plot, representing all higher levels. Threshold heights are in feet above each station’s MLLW.

Palo Alto: NOAA supplies high/low predictions only. The curve uses a half-cosine interpolation between those points, for orientation; intermediate heights are not NOAA predictions.

Coverage: Up to 31 PST calendar days per load. Observations end at the present; missing samples and future deltas stay blank. Recent observations may be preliminary and can change. Tooltips and CSV include NOAA quality information.

Palo Alto · MLLW → NAVD88

NAVD88 heights are approximately 1–1.5 ft lower than the local MLLW heights shown on this chart.

NAVD88 ≈ chart height − 1 to 1.5 ft

For example, 10 ft MLLW is approximately 8.5–9 ft NAVD88.

Approximate conversion. The chart and its threshold bands remain in local MLLW.

Flood impacts · NAVD88

Alviso

  • At about 13 ft NAVD88, flooding may occur. Equates roughly to a 100-year coastal flood event + 2 ft SLR.

Palo Alto

At 10.8 ft NAVD88 (roughly, a king tide + 2 ft SLR):

  • Airfield: 2 ft depth flooding; some buildings flooded.

At 11.9 ft NAVD88 (roughly, a 10-year coastal flood event with 2 ft SLR):

  • Airfield floods to a significant depth of 5–8 ft.
  • Most buildings north of 101 have a depth of about 1–2 ft. These appear to be office buildings, not residences.

At 13.02 ft NAVD88 (about a 100-year coastal flood event with 2 ft SLR):

  • Airfield: about 10 ft depth of water.
  • Most buildings north of 101 would flood to a depth of up to 5 ft.
  • Flooding starts to spread south of 101, with some residences flooding.

SLR = sea level rise. All impact elevations above use NAVD88; the tide charts use local MLLW.
